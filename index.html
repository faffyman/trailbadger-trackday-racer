      How to play

      The race

      You are Brock the badger. You race Tod the fox, Bruin the bear and Clover the rabbit. The first rider to finish all the laps wins. Staying on the dirt track keeps you fast. The grass slows you down.

      Touch controls

      • Hold the big button on the right to pedal.
      • Hold the arrows on the left to steer.
      • Tap the small button to brake.
      • You can use both thumbs at the same time.

      Keyboard controls

      • Left and Right arrow keys, or A and D, steer.
      • Up arrow or W pedals.
      • Down arrow or S brakes.
      • Escape or P pauses the race.
      • Enter starts and confirms.

      Pickups

      • Lightning bolt. Gives you a short burst of speed.
      • Mud splat. Drops mud behind you. A rider who rides through it slows down. Your own mud never slows you.
      • Thorny ball. Drops brambles behind you. A rider who hits them cannot steer for a moment and loses speed.

      Pickups come back after a few seconds.

      Jumps and berms

      A ramp launches you into the air if you ride fast enough. Land with the bike pointing the way you travel. A clean landing gives you a small boost. A bad landing slows you down.

      A berm is a banked bend. Ride on the outside of the bend. You grip better and go faster there.

      Daily tracks

      A new track appears every day. Everyone gets the same track on the same day. The game keeps the last 30 days for you to replay. The two intro tracks are gentle and are always there.
